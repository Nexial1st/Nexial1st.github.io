MUUSE: FROM BALI BEACH TO CITY-WIDE SYSTEM

A reuse platform born from frustration with beach plastic, scaled through proof points across Asia, now entering Australia.
2018
Bali — surfers against plastic
Founded as "revolv" by surfers frustrated by takeaway waste. The insight: recycling wasn't enough. They needed reuse infrastructure.
2019
Analogue pilots
Cup-exchange networks with local cafés. No app, no tech. Learning what makes people return packaging.
2020
Digital pivot → "Muuse"
Serialised containers, QR codes, chain-of-custody tracking. Grassroots campaign becomes scalable infrastructure.
2021–22
Singapore — proving the model
Our Tampines Hub (1.2t CO₂/yr saved). Hawker centre: 200k items diverted. No-deposit, late-fee behaviour model emerges.
2023–24
Hong Kong — mainstream scale
Starbucks (35 stores). Swire Properties. 1M items diverted by end 2024. Proves model in premium retail.
2025
98–99% return rates
Near-total return without deposits. ~USD 4.2M revenue. Impact data feeding partner ESG disclosures.
2026
Gold Coast — the next chapter
City-wide market entry via Haymarket HQ. Enzyme as system integrator. Aarhus model as reference. QLD bans creating push, 13M+ visitors creating pull.
