1. What we are trying to do together

This is our shared dashboard so we can look at the same information at the same time and make decisions together, not a fixed plan. The aim is to get us back in the black, keep the family steady through the new baby, and avoid locking into any path that we later regret.

Financial guardrails

  • Back in the black = no revolving bad debt and at least three months of living costs in buffer.
  • We avoid any move that obviously increases our net debt without a clear way for Enzyme income to cover it.
  • Where possible, we prefer options that lower our burn rate while keeping income potential open.

Family & timing guardrails

  • Birth near a hospital with acceptable travel time (Gold Coast University Hospital vs Royal Women’s Hospital vs Caboolture).
  • No long, inflexible leases or major setup purchases before we review everything again by February 2027.
  • No move to Vietnam until our daughter is at least six months old.

Work & Enzyme guardrails

  • Enzyme + a small number of good projects is our main engine for getting back in the black; salaried roles are tools, but usually capped, and the PhD is one helpful amplifier among several.
  • Andrew’s specialist work should be supported, not stalled, by our location choices, with the PhD acting as one useful amplifier for credibility, access to funding, and lower-friction entry into some projects.
  • We avoid any path where most of our income depends on a single client if we can.

The aim here is to remove as many open loops as possible before we land in SEQ, so our energy goes into the baby, Enzyme, and family, not unfinished business in Singapore.

4. Enzyme & work – our main engine

For us, the best way to get back in the black is to retain and grow Enzyme with a small number of good projects, supported by Katherine’s work where it makes sense, and to keep building Andrew’s specialist positioning over the next few years. The PhD matters because it can add credibility, improve access to some funding, and lower friction into certain specialist projects, but it sits within the broader Enzyme strategy rather than above it. Salaried roles are tools when needed, but usually come with capped upside.

4.1 Enzyme pipeline (illustrative)

  • Queensland / SEQ opportunities – government, tourism and hotel work that may be easier to build from a Brisbane/Gold Coast base.
  • Singapore and wider Asia – C‑suite and executive work that can still be served remotely or via trips, even if we are based in Australia or Vietnam.
  • Pacific and Hoi An / Vietnam – emerging work where a lower cost base could allow us to reinvest more into Enzyme and savings.

The location choice is therefore partly: where can we live in a way that gives Enzyme the best chance to grow, while also supporting specialist positioning and keeping our burn rate under control?

4.2 How location affects Enzyme

  • Brisbane / SEQ city-side: stronger access to Queensland clients and networks.
  • Gold Coast (Paradise Waters): lower setup cost, good lifestyle, and still connected to Brisbane by rail and road.
  • Singapore (later, if ever): closer to many decision-makers, but only makes sense again if big, signed projects cover the higher cost base.
  • Hoi An (later): lower daily costs so that each Enzyme project has a bigger impact on debt and savings, after our daughter is older.
